# Emergency and Escalation Procedure
Clinic: Greenfield Family Clinic (fictional sample)
Effective date: 1 February 2026 | Owner: Medical Director

## Medical emergencies
If a patient is experiencing a medical emergency such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, or loss of consciousness, staff must call the local emergency number immediately and alert the duty doctor. The clinic is not an emergency department. Staff must not give medical advice over the phone or by message for emergencies.

## Questions staff cannot answer
Staff and automated assistants must not give diagnoses, recommend medicines or doses, or interpret test results. These questions are referred to the duty doctor. Patients can leave a request for a call back from a doctor, which is answered within one working day.

## Complaints
Complaints can be made in person, by email or by phone. The clinic acknowledges complaints within 2 working days and aims to resolve them within 14 days. Complaints about a doctor are reviewed by the Medical Director. Complaints about billing are reviewed by the Accounts Department.

## Escalation contacts
- Billing disputes and fee waivers: Clinic Manager
- Privacy and data concerns: Data Protection Officer
- Clinical concerns: Medical Director
