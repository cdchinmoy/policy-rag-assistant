# Patient FAQ
Clinic: Greenfield Family Clinic (fictional sample)

## Do I need an appointment?
Yes. The clinic works by appointment only, except for emergencies, which should go to a hospital.

## What should I bring to my first visit?
Bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, any previous medical reports, and a list of medicines you currently take.

## Can I choose my doctor?
Yes, subject to availability. Request a specific doctor when you book.

## Can a family member book or cancel for me?
Yes, a family member can book or cancel an appointment on your behalf. They cannot receive your medical information unless you have given consent.

## Is parking available?
Free parking is available behind the building for patients, for up to 2 hours.

## Do you offer online consultations?
Yes, telephone and video consultations are available for follow-up visits and minor concerns. The doctor will decide if an in-person visit is needed.

## How do I get my reports?
Reports are available through the secure patient portal or in person at reception. They are not sent over WhatsApp or email.
