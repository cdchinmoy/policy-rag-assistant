# Privacy and Patient Records Policy
Clinic: Greenfield Family Clinic (fictional sample)
Effective date: 1 January 2026 | Owner: Data Protection Officer

## Confidentiality
Patient information is confidential. Staff may access patient records only when needed for their work. Staff must never discuss a patient's details in public areas or on personal social media.

## Sharing information
Patient information is shared with third parties only with the patient's written consent, or when required by law. Insurers receive only the information needed to process a claim. Information about an adult patient is not shared with family members unless the patient has given consent.

## Access to records
Patients may request a copy of their own records in writing. The clinic provides the copy within 10 working days. The first copy is free; each additional copy costs 200 rupees.

## Record retention
Adult patient records are kept for 7 years after the last visit. Records of patients under 18 are kept until the patient turns 25.

## Messaging and WhatsApp
Appointment reminders and general information may be sent by WhatsApp. Test results, diagnoses and other clinical details must never be sent over WhatsApp; patients are asked to collect them in person or through the secure patient portal.

## Data breaches
Any suspected data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer immediately, and no later than 24 hours after it is discovered.
