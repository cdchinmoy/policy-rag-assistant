# Appointments and Cancellations Policy
Clinic: Greenfield Family Clinic (fictional sample)
Effective date: 1 March 2026 | Owner: Clinic Manager

## Booking appointments
Appointments can be booked by phone, through the website, or at reception. New patients must complete a registration form before their first visit and arrive 15 minutes early.

## Cancellation and rescheduling
Patients must give at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Notice can be given by phone, WhatsApp message to the clinic number, or the website. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice are treated as late cancellations.

## Late cancellations and no-shows
A late cancellation is charged a fee of 20 percent of the consultation fee. A no-show (not attending without any notice) is charged 50 percent of the consultation fee. After three no-shows in a 12-month period, the clinic may require prepayment for future bookings.

## Late arrivals
If a patient arrives more than 15 minutes late, the clinic may need to reschedule the appointment. The late-cancellation fee does not apply if the clinic cannot see the patient because of its own delay.

## Waiver of fees
The Clinic Manager may waive a late-cancellation fee in cases of documented emergencies or serious illness. Staff cannot waive fees on their own; they must refer the request to the Clinic Manager.
