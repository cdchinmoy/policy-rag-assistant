# Fees and Payments Policy
Clinic: Greenfield Family Clinic (fictional sample)
Effective date: 1 March 2026 | Owner: Accounts Department

## Consultation fees
- Standard consultation (20 minutes): 800 rupees
- Extended consultation (40 minutes): 1,400 rupees
- Follow-up visit within 14 days of the original consultation: 400 rupees
- Telephone or video consultation: 600 rupees

## Accepted payment methods
The clinic accepts cash, debit and credit cards, and UPI. Cheques are not accepted. Payment is due at the time of the visit.

## Insurance
The clinic works directly with insurers on the approved list at reception. For insured patients, the patient pays the co-payment at the visit. The clinic submits the claim to the insurer within 7 days. If the insurer rejects a claim, the patient becomes responsible for the full fee.

## Refunds
Refunds are available only for services paid for but not delivered, or for billing errors. Refund requests must be made within 30 days of payment. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 working days. Refunds above 5,000 rupees require approval from the Clinic Manager.

## Outstanding balances
Invoices unpaid after 30 days receive a reminder. Unpaid balances after 60 days may lead to booking restrictions until the balance is cleared.
